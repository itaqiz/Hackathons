It placed 3rd; the [results announcement](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/softwarefindercareers_the-results-are-in-a-huge-congratulations-activity-7486427617357848577-9mDV?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAACAtp0IBn0hPM6nSZtGguknsXKqVwlAmn_Y) has the details.

## The challenge

The brief asked for a 1,500–2,000 word report on how AI is reshaping HR software purchasing decisions in 2026. Entries had to:

- draw only on sources published in 2024 or later
- support every major claim with a citation
- hold a clear, logical structure
- add original analysis rather than summarize existing coverage
- include a version history documenting the drafting process
- be fully original work

## What the report covers

- Agentic AI vs generative AI
- AI-driven HR software procurement
- Governance and explainability
- Enterprise AI adoption trends
- AI compliance and regulatory challenges
- HR software evaluation criteria
- Vendor landscape analysis
- AI governance debt
- Data readiness
- Organizational transformation
- Future purchasing strategies

## Sources

Citations draw on research and analysis from Gartner, Deloitte, SHRM, ADP, Workday, and SiliconANGLE, all published between 2024 and 2026 in line with the writeathon's sourcing requirement.
